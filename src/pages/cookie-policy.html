Cookie Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that a website saves on your phone or computer. Some are needed to make a website work. Others track what you do, for example for advertising or statistics.

Cookies on our website

Our website does not use any cookies.

We don't use tracking cookies, advertising cookies or statistics tools such as Google Analytics. We don't load anything from Facebook, Google or other companies while you browse our pages. That is why you won't see a cookie banner.

Links to other websites

Some links on our site go to other websites, such as Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, Google Maps and Google Reviews. If you click one, that website may set its own cookies. Their own cookie policies apply, not ours.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Our website will still work normally.

  • Safari
  • Firefox
  • Microsoft Edge

If this changes

If we add cookies in the future (for example, to count visitors), we will update this page and ask for your permission first where the law requires it.
