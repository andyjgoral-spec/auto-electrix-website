Our Customer Journey

Our expertise lies in resolving complex electrical faults that require advanced diagnostic techniques and specialised knowledge.

We focus on providing accurate, long-term solutions rather than quick, temporary fixes. This thorough approach ensures your vehicle is properly repaired the first time. We handle the challenging auto-electrical problems that other garages can’t solve. Our specialised diagnostic process involves comprehensive analysis using professional-grade equipment and manufacturer-approved methods. Quality diagnostics take time and expertise – but they deliver lasting solutions.

How does the diagnostic process work?

Step 1: Initial Contact

When you contact us, complete the Initial Assessment Enquiry form, and describe what symptoms you’re experiencing with your vehicle. Don’t worry if you’re not sure what’s causing the problem – that’s exactly what we’re here for. Our expertise lies in translating your car’s symptoms into an accurate diagnosis.

Step 2: Professional Assessment

We come to you, wherever your vehicle is in the Bournemouth area, and examine it using advanced diagnostic techniques. Your car communicates through data, warning lights, and system behaviours – we’re fluent in this “language” and know how to interpret what it’s really telling us.

Step 3: Expert Fault Finding

As auto-electrical specialists, we understand that every fault is unique. We systematically analyse data from all relevant modules to pinpoint the exact cause of the problem, not just the symptoms.

What makes our approach different?
  • We don’t guess – we use scientific diagnostic methods
  • We understand fault codes properly – a fault code indicates a sensor reading outside normal parameters, but the sensor itself is rarely the actual problem
  • We find root causes – not just symptoms
What equipment do we use?

Our diagnostic arsenal includes:

  • Manufacturer-approved wiring diagrams specific to your vehicle
  • Professional oscilloscopes for electrical analysis
  • Specialised test benches
  • Fault simulation equipment
  • Advanced diagnostic software
What are our diagnostic rates?

Initial assessments, diagnostics and fault finding are charged at £100 per hour.

Programming and coding are charged at specialist rates. Please contact us for a quote.

  • Advanced equipment and manufacturer-approved resources
  • Fix-it-right-first-time philosophy
