ADAS Calibration – Bournemouth

Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) calibration is a critical safety procedure that ensures vehicle safety technologies function accurately and reliably after repairs, windscreen replacements, or component installations.

How ADAS Calibration Works

This sophisticated process involves precisely aligning and configuring sensors, cameras, radar units, and LiDAR systems that power features like adaptive cruise control, lane departure warnings, automatic emergency braking, and blind spot monitoring.

The Risks of Incorrect ADAS Calibration

ADAS calibration requires specialised equipment, manufacturer-specific software, and controlled environments to achieve the exact positioning and targeting parameters necessary for optimal system performance. The process typically involves both static calibration (performed in a workshop using specific targets and patterns) and dynamic calibration (conducted through real-world driving scenarios), ensuring that safety systems can accurately detect obstacles, measure distances, and respond appropriately to road conditions. Without proper calibration, these life-saving technologies may malfunction, providing false warnings, failing to activate when needed, or creating dangerous driving situations.

Why Professional ADAS Calibration Matters

As vehicles become increasingly reliant on ADAS technology, professional calibration services have become essential for maintaining road safety and ensuring compliance with manufacturer specifications and legal requirements.

This type of specialised ADAS calibration expertise is becoming increasingly crucial as more vehicles incorporate advanced safety features.
