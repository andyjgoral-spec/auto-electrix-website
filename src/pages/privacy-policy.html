2. What information we collect

When you fill in a form on our website, we collect what you type in. This can include:

  • your name, phone number, email address and how you prefer us to contact you
  • your vehicle details, such as make, model, registration, mileage and engine type
  • where your vehicle is, so we can come to you
  • details of the problem with your vehicle, and any work done on it before
  • any photos or files you choose to upload
  • your preferred appointment date

When you phone, email or WhatsApp us, or when we visit you, we keep the details you give us so we can deal with your enquiry and carry out work on your vehicle.

When we do work for you, we keep job records, quotes, invoices and payment records.

We do not ask for sensitive information (such as health or religious beliefs). Please don't include it in your messages.

4. Who we share it with

We only share your information when we need to. This includes:

  • Our website host, Cloudflare, Inc., which runs our website and passes your form submissions on to our email.
  • Our email and IT providers, which store the messages we send and receive.
  • Our accountant, and HMRC when the law requires it.
  • Parts suppliers, manufacturers or recovery companies, but only when needed for your job. For example, a manufacturer may need your vehicle's registration or VIN to supply software or parts.
  • The police, courts or regulators, if the law requires us to.

5. Information sent outside the UK

Cloudflare is based in the United States, so information sent through our website forms may pass through its servers outside the UK. When this happens, it is protected by legal safeguards approved by the UK government, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK–US "data bridge".

7. Keeping it safe

Our website uses a secure connection (HTTPS). Only people who need your information to do their job can see it. We use trusted providers that have strong security in place.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • see the information we hold about you (a "subject access request")
  • correct information that is wrong
  • delete your information, unless we have to keep it by law
  • limit how we use it, or object to us using it
  • move your information to another company in a common format
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we are relying on your consent

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 1. It is free, and we will reply within one month.

9. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have used your information, please tell us first so we can put it right.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113.

10. Links to other websites

Our website links to Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp and Google. Those companies have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for them.
